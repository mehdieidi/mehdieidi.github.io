Primary source + research wing

Grady Booch · The History of Software Engineering · extended to 2026

Every major section of the seven-page essay remains mapped to a physical gallery. The later galleries are explicitly labeled as a researched extension, so Booch’s 2018 claims and the museum’s 2019–2026 history are never silently blended.

Open original PDF ↗7 pages · IEEE Software, Sept/Oct 2018

The research wing uses institutional histories, standards bodies, official project records and empirical research. Each research exhibit also links directly to the sources supporting its historical claims.

The museum is grounded in Booch’s essay, but a small number of dates, attributions and spellings are visibly corrected or expanded rather than silently repeated.

The essay closes with acknowledgments, ten references and a short author biography. They are preserved here so the museum covers the paper’s documentary apparatus as well as its narrative.
